Settings

Click the ground and your character plans a route there, jumping over steps, gaps and platforms by itself. Works while no tool is equipped; any move key cancels it. Camera distance: scroll out from first person into third person (like Roblox) and back in.
V-Sync on = the game draws once per screen refresh. Off = it draws as fast as it can (uses more CPU); the FPS limit still applies. On: items (in your hand and on the ground) and the spawn force-field are real 3D models. Off: the classic flat sprites and bubble.
Collisions on: players and the bot are solid and block each other. In multiplayer the host controls the bot.
In multiplayer this is the round-trip time to your friend; alone it is the local loopback delay.
Draws the exact route click-to-walk follows: green = walking, pink = a jump (orange = sprint jump), blue = the surfaces it stands on. Hitboxes: cyan = you, red = the bot, orange = the other player. Dim box = wall collision, bright parts = the real body that blocks other characters. Data sizes of world, rendering, lighting and physics, plus RAM / CPU / GPU. Browsers don't expose true CPU or GPU load, so those are estimates.
